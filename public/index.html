Official Teen Patti Rules

Standard Indian 3 Patti Ranking Hierarchy & Rules

1. Trail / Trio / Set (Highest): Three cards of identical rank. A-A-A is highest, 2-2-2 is lowest.
2. Pure Sequence / Straight Flush: 3 consecutive cards of SAME suit. In Teen Patti, A-2-3 is the highest sequence, followed by A-K-Q, K-Q-J down to 4-3-2.
3. Sequence / Normal Run: 3 consecutive cards of DIFFERENT suits. A-2-3 is highest, followed by A-K-Q down to 4-3-2.
4. Color / Flush: 3 cards of SAME suit. Compared by highest card (A-K-J > A-K-10).
5. Pair: 2 cards of same rank. Pair of Aces (A-A-K) beats Pair of Kings (K-K-Q).
6. High Card: No combination. Evaluated by highest card down to lowest.

Blind vs Seen Rules:

• Blind Player: Bets 1x or 2x stake (up to 4 blind rounds).

• Seen Player: Bets 2x or 4x stake (double blind amount).

• Sideshow: A Seen player can request sideshow with previous seen player.

• Show: Allowed when only 2 active players remain in hand.
